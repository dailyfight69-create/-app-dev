## My Favorite Series: JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure

*JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure* is a long-running Japanese manga and anime series created by Hirohiko Araki. The series is known for its unique art style, creative battles, and memorable characters. It follows the adventures of the Joestar family across multiple generations, each with a protagonist nicknamed "JoJo."

### Why I Love JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure
- **Unique Art Style:** The characters and poses are iconic and instantly recognizable.
- **Creative Powers:** The series features "Stands," supernatural manifestations of fighting spirit with a wide variety of abilities.
- **Epic Storytelling:** Each part has its own unique tone, setting, and villain, keeping the series fresh and exciting.
- **Memorable Characters:** From Jonathan Joestar to Jotaro Kujo and beyond, each JoJo leaves a lasting impression.

### Main Parts of JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure:
1. **Phantom Blood** - The origin story featuring Jonathan Joestar.
2. **Battle Tendency** - Follows Joseph Joestar’s adventures.
3. **Stardust Crusaders** - Introduces the Stand concept and Jotaro Kujo.
4. **Diamond is Unbreakable** - Focuses on Josuke Higashikata and the town of Morioh.
